# 03 · Higharc

**Slice:** production-home BIM, options, takeoff  
**Site:** [higharc.com](https://www.higharc.com/)  
**Note:** Series C reported 2026 (~$95M). Closest commercial cousin to a full home-builder design platform.

## What it does

Sketch or option a production house into a BIM-native model. Generative Building Model (2026) tokenizes rooms so layouts keep geometry, not pixels. Downstream: options, framing counts, buyer configuration.
