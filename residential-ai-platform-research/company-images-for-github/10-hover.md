# 10 · HOVER

**Slice:** phone photos → measured 3D + quantities  
**Site:** [hover.to](https://hover.to/)  
**API:** [developers.hover.to](https://developers.hover.to)

## What it does

Walk around a house, shoot photos. HOVER returns exterior (and some interior) measurements as JSON / PDF / XLSX / SketchUp. First-class measurement API + webhooks.

## Product still

![HOVER phone photo to measured 3D house](images/hover-photo-to-model.jpg)

Phone capture → white 3D envelope with trim / ridge / eave counts. Source: Qualified Remodeler product page.

## Fits our map

- As-built dimensions without a survey crew
- Remodel takeoff
- Exterior envelope for insurance / siding / roofing

## Limits

Not a generative designer. Not a live sensor twin. Interior completeness varies by product tier — verify before promising full inside measurements.
