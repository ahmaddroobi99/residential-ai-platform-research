# 06 · Maket.ai

**Slice:** natural-language residential plans  
**Site:** [maket.ai](https://www.maket.ai/)

## What it does

Type a brief (beds, baths, area, style). Get an editable floor plan, then 3D / renders. Upload PDF/JPG plans on paid tiers. Builder platform turns catalog 2D into 3D for homebuyers.

## Limits
