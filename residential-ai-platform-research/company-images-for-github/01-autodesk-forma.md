# 01 · Autodesk Forma

**Slice:** land, climate, massing, BIM handoff  
**Site:** [autodesk.com/products/forma](https://www.autodesk.com/products/forma/overview)  
**Was:** Spacemaker (acquired 2020, brand retired May 2023). Do not list Spacemaker as current.

## What it does

Early-site design: context buildings, proposals, sun / wind / noise / operational energy. Syncs into Revit. Forma Building Design added AI layout exploration in 2026.
