# 09 · Matterport (CoStar)

**Slice:** reality capture / digital twin  
**Site:** [matterport.com](https://matterport.com/)

## What it does

Camera or phone → dollhouse + walkthrough + schematic plan + point cloud / MatterPak. Used in real estate and construction. Embeds exist for Procore / ACC.

## Fits our map

- As-built vs as-designed
- Digital twin capture
- Inspection / marketing handover

## Limits

A pretty scan is not IFC. You still need a scan-to-BIM step if you want typed walls and spaces.
