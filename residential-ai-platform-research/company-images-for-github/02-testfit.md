# 02 · TestFit

**Slice:** site yield + deal math  
**Site:** [testfit.io](https://www.testfit.io/)  
**HQ:** Dallas, USA. Founded 2016.

## What it does

Drop a site and a typology (multifamily, parking, industrial, hotel). Get unit count, parking, efficiency, and a live pro forma while you drag the building.

## Product still

![TestFit multifamily yield and cost summary](images/testfit-feasibility.jpg)

Unit mix, NOI, land / soft / hard costs, yield-on-cost. Source: TestFit multifamily page.

## Fits our map

- Parcel packing
- Parking + coverage
- Financial feasibility before design

## Limits

Stronger on multifamily and parking than custom single-family interiors. Not BIM authoring. Not a twin.
